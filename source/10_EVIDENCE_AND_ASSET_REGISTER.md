# 10 — Evidence and Asset Register

## Evidence principles

- Preserve originals; work from copies.
- Record provenance, date, owner and permission.
- Scan both front and back of physical items.
- Export emails with headers where relevant.
- Do not alter screenshots beyond redacting private data.
- Create cryptographic checksums for important files.
- Separate public, private, confidential and legally restricted assets.
