## Language style

Saiffil prefers:

- English for technical precision;
- natural Bahasa Melayu Malaysia for broad public communication;
- balanced tone, neither overly formal nor careless;
- useful analogies;
- direct language;
- confident but evidence-based claims.

He dislikes:

- generic corporate filler;
- Indonesian phrasing presented as Malaysian Malay;
- shallow hype;
- invented certainty;
- boring, template-like creative work;
- jargon used to conceal weak understanding.

## Words requiring caution

- expert;
- visionary;
- pioneer;
- first;
- world-class;
- guru;
- empire;
- award-winning;
- revolutionary;
- leading;
- largest.

Use only with evidence or quotation.
