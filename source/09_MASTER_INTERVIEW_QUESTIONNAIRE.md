# 09 — Master Interview Questionnaire

Record every interview in audio and transcript form. Ask for dates, places, names, documents and witnesses after each story.

## A. Family of origin

1. Where were you born and where did you spend your first ten years?
2. What were your parents like in private?
3. What work did they do?
4. What did your home feel like?
5. Who gave you the Whiz Kid?
6. Who gave you the PC in 1996?
7. Was buying a computer financially difficult for the family?
8. What did your parents think you would become?

10. Who thought you were wasting time?
11. What family rules shaped you?
12. How was faith practised at home?
13. What did money mean in your family?
