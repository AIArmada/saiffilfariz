## Research sequence

### Phase 1 — Evidence preservation

Collect files before links, accounts, devices and memories disappear.

### Phase 2 — Saiffil master interviews

Conduct at least eight two-hour interviews, separated by era and theme.

### Phase 3 — Family interviews

Interview parents, siblings, partner/co-parent and adult-capable children under clear consent.

### Phase 4 — Witness interviews

At least two independent witnesses per major era where possible.

### Phase 5 — Corporate and campaign verification

Normalise entities, roles, numbers, contracts and dates.

### Phase 6 — Adversarial review

Ask trusted sceptics and former collaborators what the current narrative gets wrong.

### Phase 7 — Narrative development

Only after evidence and contradictions are mapped should the ghostwriter commit to a final book structure.

## Contradiction log template

For every contradiction, record:

- statement A;
- source A;
- statement B;
- source B;
- possible explanation;
- person to interview;
- document needed;
- current publication decision.

## Research standard

A biography does not fail because some facts remain uncertain. It fails when uncertainty is hidden.
