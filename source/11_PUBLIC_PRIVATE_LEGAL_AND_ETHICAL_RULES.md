# 11 — Public, Private, Legal and Ethical Rules

## 1. Default status

This pack is private. A ghostwriter may use it for research but may not assume every detail belongs in the book.

## 2. Never publish automatically

- national identity numbers;
- private addresses;
- personal telephone numbers;
- passwords, security details or account identifiers;
- children's full personal details;
- medical records;
- accident details;
- private religious confessions;
- relationship history;
- internal financial records;
- disputes;
- confidential contracts;
- employee data.

## 3. Children

Use a separate consent protocol. Consider present age, future digital permanence and whether the scene belongs to the child's life rather than the father's narrative.

## 4. Health and accident

Do not sensationalise injury, memory loss or recovery. Verify medical terminology. Allow Saiffil to set boundaries and review final passages.

## 5. Religion

Do not infer authority, doctrine or piety. Use first-person reflection and verified practice. Avoid turning faith into commercial positioning.

## 6. CellMaxx health claims

Do not reproduce historic medical, wellness, stem-cell, therapeutic or disease claims without legal and scientific review. The biography can discuss branding, commerce, distribution and historical context without endorsing product claims.

## 7. Commercial metrics

Obtain documentary evidence and define every number. Use RM amounts exactly; do not convert unless the publication requires it and the original value remains visible.

## 8. Other people's reputations

Offer meaningful subjects a right of reply where allegations, disputes or negative judgments appear. Separate memory, opinion and documentary fact.

## 9. Copyright and archives

Secure permissions for photographs, advertisements, aircraft images, magazine pages, logos, screenshots, music, video and third-party writing.

## 10. Ghostwriter confidentiality

Use secure storage, least-privilege access, encrypted backups and a written destruction/return policy for source material.

## 11. Ethical standard

The biography should neither sanitise the life into propaganda nor expose private pain merely to make the book more marketable.
