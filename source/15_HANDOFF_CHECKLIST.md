# 15 — Handoff Checklist

A ghostwriter receiving this pack should complete the following before drafting chapters.

## Orientation

- [ ] Read the entire pack.
- [ ] Read both reference documents.
- [ ] Review the structured CSV/JSON data.
- [ ] Sign confidentiality and data-handling terms.
- [ ] Confirm publication audience, length, language and legal jurisdiction.

## Source control

- [ ] Create a source database.
- [ ] Assign every claim a source and confidence status.
- [ ] Build a contradiction log.
- [ ] Build a permissions log.
- [ ] Separate private and public archives.

## Interviews

- [ ] Schedule master interviews with Saiffil.
- [ ] Create family consent plan.
- [ ] Build witness list.
- [ ] Interview people who may disagree.
- [ ] Transcribe and timestamp all recordings.

## Evidence

- [ ] Preserve old devices and media.
- [ ] Archive websites and social accounts.
- [ ] Verify corporate records.
- [ ] Verify financial and scale claims.
- [ ] Verify campaign claims.
- [ ] Identify image and copyright owners.

## Editorial

- [ ] Choose provisional architecture.
- [ ] Identify opening and closing scenes.
- [ ] Define central question and tensions.
- [ ] Avoid corporate-profile tone.
- [ ] Distinguish achievement, interpretation and legacy lesson.
- [ ] Submit sensitive chapters for subject review.

## Completion standard

The manuscript is not ready merely because it contains all known facts. It is ready when it also contains:

- relationships;
- consequence;
- conflict;
- emotion;
- uncertainty;
- ordinary life;
- other people's perspectives;
- and enough evidence for a reader to trust the larger pattern.
