# 00 — Start Here

## The biography this project should produce

The goal is not to produce a long corporate profile. It is to produce a biography with enough depth that Saiffil's children, collaborators, readers and future generations can understand:

- what happened;
- what he tried to build;
- how he thought;
- who influenced him;
- who stood beside him;
- what success demanded;
- what failure or disruption cost;
- what he learned;
- what he believed;
- what remained unfinished;
- and what he hoped others would carry forward.

## The central biographical thesis

Saiffil's life repeatedly trained him to **connect what others separate**.

He moved through:

- machines;
- code;
- networks;
- servers;
- digital media;
- marketplaces;
- payments;
- custom software;
- business operations;
- organisational scale;
- brand visibility;
- learning;
- faith;
- fatherhood;
- and artificial intelligence.

The strongest biography will not treat these as unrelated adventures. It will show how each environment formed a capability that reappeared in the next.

## What the biography is not

It must not reduce him to:

- an AI speaker;
- a programmer;
- a businessman;
- a Maxxima founder;
- the CellMaxx/AirAsia story;
- a motivational personality;
- a nostalgic computer enthusiast;
- or a person who claims expertise in everything.

Each description contains a fragment, but none contains the whole.

## The present-day synthesis

A useful internal description is:

> Saiffil Fariz is a programmer-entrepreneur, systems builder and educator whose unusual strength is connecting technical mechanisms, commercial reality, influence, learning and human judgment.

Current public positioning may use:

> **AI Transformation Strategist · Systems Builder · Entrepreneur · Educator**

A more distinctive category under development is:

> **Connected Intelligence Strategist**

These are positioning tools, not the biography's final verdict.

## The emotional promise

The biography should let readers experience several versions of Saiffil:

- the child fascinated by a machine that answered back;
- the young troubleshooter refusing to surrender to a broken PC;
- the student discovering the terminal and remote machines;
- the early web builder entering a borderless world;
- the entrepreneur turning code into commerce;
- the operator learning that demand can burn hardware and scale can expose weakness;
- the leader inside a large commercial network;
- the brand builder bold enough to put a product on an aircraft;
- the person confronting disruption, uncertainty and reinvention;
- the father thinking about what his children should inherit;
- the learner using AI to compress the distance between curiosity and capability;
- and the seeker asking what intelligence is ultimately for.

## The primary tensions

A biography becomes alive through tension, not a sequence of achievements.

The main tensions available here are:

1. **Curiosity versus certainty** — he often entered before knowing everything.
2. **Self-learning versus formal credentials** — he studied Computer Engineering but built much of his capability outside formal completion.
3. **Builder versus public figure** — much of his confidence came from making systems work, not from performing expertise.
4. **Technology versus people** — software behaves differently from organisations.
5. **Speed versus structure** — early action created opportunities and exposed fragility.
6. **Scale versus control** — growth magnified both capability and weakness.
7. **Visibility versus substance** — branding could move attention, but systems had to fulfil the promise.
8. **Ambition versus responsibility** — power, markets and AI require judgment.
9. **Achievement versus cost** — success stories may conceal exhaustion, pressure, setbacks and sacrifice.
10. **Reinvention versus legacy** — he continues building while trying to preserve what the journey means.

## The ghostwriter's first obligation

Do not improve the story by inventing certainty.

Where evidence is incomplete:

- mark it;
- ask;
- verify;
- preserve multiple versions;
- and write only what the evidence supports.

The subject explicitly dislikes confident incompleteness.
