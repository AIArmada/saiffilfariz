# Saiffil Fariz Biography Source Pack

## Purpose

This folder is a **ghostwriter-ready research environment** for writing a serious, complete biography of **Saiffil Fariz bin Mohamad Yusuff**.

It is not a finished biography and not a publicity brochure. It is designed so that a new ghostwriter can enter the project, understand the known life, see the unresolved questions, recognise the narrative possibilities, protect private information, and continue research without restarting from zero.

The pack separates five different things that are often confused:

1. **Known facts** — what Saiffil has directly stated or what existing materials document.
2. **Claims requiring verification** — numbers, dates, titles and “first” claims that need evidence.
3. **Interpretation** — what an event may mean in the story.
4. **Private material** — relevant to legacy, but not automatically suitable for publication.
5. **Narrative possibilities** — ways a ghostwriter might shape the material without inventing it.

## Critical warning

Do not confuse comprehensiveness with certainty.

This pack is comprehensive **relative to currently available conversations and documents**. It cannot contain memories, relationships, private episodes, feelings or evidence that have not yet been provided. The interview and evidence plans are therefore part of the source pack, not an afterthought.

## One-line editorial principle

> Write the human being, not the résumé; prove the achievements, do not worship them; and reveal the pattern connecting the eras.
