# 02 — Fact Ledger and Verification

## Status definitions

- **CONFIRMED:** directly stated by Saiffil or repeatedly established.
- **DOCUMENTED:** present in associated material, but still subject to source quality.
- **VERIFY:** exact wording, number, date, entity or scope needs evidence.
- **INTERPRETATION:** useful meaning, not a literal fact.
- **PRIVATE:** relevant to legacy, not automatically publishable.
- **CONFLICT:** two or more versions exist.

## Golden rules

1. Never convert a remembered number into a verified statistic.
2. Never merge related claims simply because they sound compatible.
3. Distinguish a personal influence from a personal mentor or acquaintance.
4. Distinguish programme concept, planned event and completed event.
5. Distinguish company, brand, legal entity and campaign.
6. Preserve the source wording until verification is complete.
7. Record the date on which a fact was true.
8. Do not publish children's information without explicit consent and purpose.
9. Do not publish health, accident, religious or financial details merely because they make the story dramatic.
