# SAIFFIL FARIZ PERSONAL WEBSITE
## Master Story, Brand, Content and Build Brief

> This document is the working source of truth for an agent or team designing and building Saiffil Fariz's personal website.
>
> It is not merely a biography. It defines what the website must communicate, what Saiffil's history means, which outcomes should become strengths, what visitors should perceive, how proof should be organised, how the website should feel and behave, what must never be invented, and how technology should support the story rather than replace it.

---

# 0. Read This First

## 0.1 The most important correction

Do **not** build the website around Saiffil's childhood chronology.

The Whiz Kid, floppy disks, PC repair, websites from the 1990s, servers, FTP, e-commerce, Maxxima, CellMaxx and AirAsia are valuable. However, they are **evidence**, not the main proposition.

The homepage must lead with the strength produced by those experiences:

> **Saiffil connects technology, systems, business, influence, learning and human judgment to help people and organisations move with greater clarity.**

The main website is about what Saiffil can see, connect, build and change **today**.

His personal history should explain why that capability is credible.

## 0.2 The website must follow this hierarchy

1. **Outcome and value**
2. **Distinctive perspective**
3. **Relevant proof**
4. **Methods and ideas**
5. **Current work**
6. **Personal history and origin**
7. **Formal biography**
8. **Contact or engagement**

Do not reverse this hierarchy.

## 0.3 The central creative principle

> **The website should not merely describe connected intelligence. It should behave like connected intelligence.**

This means the experience should reveal relationships between AI, software, systems, operations, commerce, media, branding, audiences, learning, leadership, people, judgment, character and outcomes.

Do not reduce the idea to five decorative cards or a colourful diagram.

Directly stated by Saiffil or repeatedly established in his materials.

Use publicly when the source is sufficiently strong and current.

Likely true and usually based on Saiffil's own account, but the exact number, date, legal entity, wording, geography, duration or category requires confirmation.

Do not silently turn these into unqualified claims.

A useful conclusion drawn from multiple experiences.

## 1.5 PRIVATE / SENSITIVE

Useful for understanding Saiffil, but not automatically suitable for public publication.

Do not publish without explicit approval.

## 1.6 HISTORICAL SNAPSHOT

Accurate for a past period but not necessarily current.

Show the relevant year or period.

# 5. The Five-Lens Model

The existing five-lens framework is useful, but it should be treated as a thinking system, not a set of service cards.

## 5.1 Code

### Meaning

How the capability is built.

Includes software, data, models, APIs, infrastructure, interfaces, automation, reliability, security and maintainability.

### Central question

> Can it work—and can it keep working?

## 5.2 Commerce

### Meaning

How the capability creates and sustains value.

Includes customer need, pricing, distribution, operations, revenue, cost, adoption, scalability and business model.

### Central question

> Why should this exist, and how will it sustain itself?

## 5.3 Influence

### Meaning

How people understand, trust, remember and act.

Includes branding, communication, media, storytelling, marketing, behaviour, reputation and customer experience.

### Central question

> Why will people notice, believe and move?

## 5.4 Learning

### Meaning

How people and organisations become more capable.

Includes curiosity, exploration, training, experimentation, reflection, practice, knowledge transfer and adaptation.

### Central question

> What capability remains after the session, system or intervention ends?

## 5.5 Character

### Meaning

How power is directed.

Includes judgment, responsibility, ethics, intention, governance, humility, resilience and human consequences.

### Central question

> Just because we can, should we—and under what conditions?

## 5.6 The integration

The website should show that real transformation happens where the lenses meet.

For example:

> An AI product can be technically impressive, commercially weak, poorly trusted, difficult to learn and irresponsibly governed.

Saiffil's value is the ability to see those interactions.

# 24. Advanced Technology Direction

The site should use modern technology where it creates an experience that HTML and CSS alone cannot produce.

## 24.1 Recommended stack

- React or Next.js with TypeScript;
- Tailwind CSS 4;
- accessible component primitives;
- Three.js through React Three Fiber where suitable;
- GSAP ScrollTrigger for deliberate scroll-linked sequences;
- Lenis only when smooth scrolling remains accessible and does not break native behaviour;
- MDX or a structured CMS for the Living Index;
- static generation or server rendering for content pages;
- optimised media pipeline;
- analytics and consent appropriate to the deployment context.

## 24.2 WebGL purpose

Use WebGL for connected spatial systems, evolving relationships, subtle depth, lens interactions, proof or data constellations and a persistent visual world.

Do not use WebGL as a screensaver, excuse for slow loading, substitute for content, novelty driving game or full-canvas barrier between the visitor and the information.

## 24.3 Progressive enhancement

All primary content must remain in semantic HTML.

The website must remain usable when WebGL is unavailable, JavaScript is slow, reduced motion is enabled, the device is low-powered, data saving is active or a screen reader is used.

## 24.4 Mobile

Do not shrink the desktop experience.

Create deliberate mobile behaviour:

- shorter sequences;
- simplified geometry;
- lower particle count;
- controlled pixel ratio;
- no hover dependency;
- tap-friendly controls;
- compressed navigation;
- preserved story priority;
- static or video fallback where appropriate.

## 24.5 Performance targets

The agent should establish and enforce budgets for initial JavaScript, route-level chunks, images, WebGL assets, fonts, Largest Contentful Paint, Interaction to Next Paint and cumulative layout shift.

Do not allow a creative feature to make the site feel slow or fragile.

# 36. Acceptance Criteria

The website is ready only when the following are true.

## Strategy

- The hero communicates present-day value.
- The site does not lead with childhood.
- The distinctive combination is clear.
- Proof supports claims.
- Audiences can find relevant paths.

## Content

- Facts are status-labelled internally.
- Unverified claims are hidden or qualified.
- No invented achievements appear.
- The voice is mainly first and second person.
- The formal bio is copy-ready.

## Design

- The site feels authored.
- The layout is not a generic component-library composition.
- The visual metaphor supports connected intelligence.
- Authentic evidence has visual priority.
- Mobile feels intentionally designed.

## Technology

- Semantic content exists without WebGL.
- The advanced visual layer performs smoothly.
- Reduced-motion and low-power fallbacks work.
- Routes are shareable and indexable.
- Content can be updated without rebuilding everything.
- No unnecessary dependency has been introduced.

## Accessibility

- Keyboard navigation works.
- Focus is visible.
- Canvas content has text equivalents.
- Forms are labelled.
- Motion can be reduced.
- Colour contrast is sufficient.

## Performance

- The hero becomes useful quickly.
- Creative technology does not block reading.
- Images and fonts are optimised.
- Route-level loading is controlled.
- Mobile performance is acceptable on mid-range devices.

## Conversion

- Event organisers know how to book.
- Organisations know how to describe a problem.
- Technical collaborators know where to engage.
- Visitors can understand the proof.
- Every CTA has a clear destination.

# 37. Final Instruction to the Building Agent

Build this website from first principles.

Do not begin by selecting a template.

Do not begin with animation.

Do not begin with the childhood timeline.

Begin with the question:

> **What should a visitor understand about Saiffil that is both valuable and difficult to imitate?**

The answer is not that he has done many things.

The answer is that those experiences created a rare ability to connect technical mechanism, business reality, human behaviour, learning, influence and responsibility.

The website must make that strength visible, believable and useful.

Use biography as evidence.

Use technology as expression.

Use proof as trust.

Use interaction to reveal thinking.

Use design to create hierarchy.

Use restraint to protect credibility.

And make the final experience worthy of the central idea:

# **Connect Intelligence to What Matters.**
