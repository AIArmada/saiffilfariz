# 04 — Story and Scene Bank

A biography is remembered through scenes. The entries below are **scene scaffolds**, not completed prose. A ghostwriter must interview Saiffil and witnesses before supplying sensory detail, dialogue or emotion.

## Scene 1 — The machine that answered back

**Known:** a Whiz Kid personal computer appears in Saiffil's earliest technology memory.

**Potential scene:** a child discovers that pressing a key produces a response.

**Questions:** Who gave it? Where was the family living? What did it look and sound like? What was the first program or card? Did anyone notice his fascination?

**Meaning:** curiosity, agency, the relationship between instruction and outcome.

## Scene 2 — The open computer case

**Known:** he dismantled and reassembled computers and upgraded hardware.

**Potential scene:** screws, cables, boards, fear of not being able to put the machine back together.

**Meaning:** mechanisms are knowable; fear decreases when systems are opened.

## Scene 3 — Disk 7 of 12

**Known:** installed operating systems and software from floppy disks, CDs and DVDs.

**Potential scene:** long installations, a corrupted disk, missing driver, starting over.

**Meaning:** patience, sequence, procedural discipline, frustration tolerance.

## Scene 4 — First website, 1996

**Known:** began building websites around 1996.

**Needs:** exact machine, connection, browser, editor, site purpose, reaction when first viewed online.

**Meaning:** the computer becomes a publishing and distribution system.

## Scene 5 — School website recognition

**Known:** remembered school website and possible award around 2000.

**Needs:** school, competition, certificate, teachers and classmates.

**Meaning:** private obsession receives public validation.

## Scene 6 — The terminal at Arizona

**Known:** Computer Engineering, terminal, SSH, C/C++/Java.

**Meaning:** distance collapses; infrastructure becomes accessible.

## Scene 7 — The accident

**Ethical requirement:** publish only with explicit approval and no sensationalism.
